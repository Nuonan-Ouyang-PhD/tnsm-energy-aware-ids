\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclude}
The pairwise non-degeneracy criterion predicts when reward normalization prevents useful IDS model switching, while matched-static attribution distinguishes adaptation from detector choice. In our frozen study, Tabular-Q's apparent primary saving is explained by its LightLR operating point; off support its deterministic tie rule selects TinyDT and reaches 67.76\% FPR. By contrast, DeadlineGuard uses a direct causal load signal to save 43.7~J against Static-MedRF (2.98\%; 95\% CI 41.4--46.0~J) and remove all 512 deadline misses at a 1.373-pp F1 cost. Learned orchestration should be credited only after reward attainability, realized actions, matched controls, service feasibility, off-support defaults, and whole-device measurement are audited together.
