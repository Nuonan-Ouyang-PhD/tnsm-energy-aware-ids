\section{Evidence and timing boundary}
The main follow-up consists of 60 accepted runs in ten six-method blocks, two invalid attempts, and 25 frozen training artifacts. Blocks 1 and 5 span recovery periods. This supplement is an editing draft, not a final release certificate.

\section{Immediate pairwise preference lemma}
For $U_a(s)=w_DD_a(s)-C_a$, $w_D>0$ and support-fixed $C_a$, let $\delta(s)=D_a(s)-D_b(s)$ and $\tau=(C_a-C_b)/w_D$. Then $U_a-U_b=w_D(\delta-\tau)$, so strict reversals over attainable support require and, when extrema are attained, are implied by $\min\delta<\tau<\max\delta$. Equality gives a tie, not a strict reversal. If extrema are not attained, state the condition in terms of existence of values on both sides rather than silently assuming attainment. Load/state-dependent costs require state-dependent thresholds. This statement does not involve transition probabilities, discounting or long-horizon value and proves no global optimality or necessary learned collapse.

\section{Reward audit procedure}
Freeze the objective, cost provenance and training/validation artifact hashes; compute immediate action-pair differences only on the declared validation support; record the attainable range and any ties/reversals; retain every pair including TinyDT-favouring cases. Do not inspect test to choose weights/support or call a retrospective audit pre-test. Freeze policy/checkpoint selection using validation only, then describe previously viewed historical test honestly. The code and cache bindings, not this pseudocode alone, establish what was executed.

\section{Information and training coverage}
Q/DQN use current arrivals, pre-append FIFO carryover, prior selected-detector mean probability and prior action. DeadlineGuard uses current work count and calibrated fixed MedRF p95. Cascade receives current-row confidence only after LightLR inference. Static controls ignore observations. No online truth, cell identity or future arrival is a feature. Queue is constant in the training surrogate; queue ablation is not identifiable and was not executed. Temperature is safety-only. Training uses historical TON power proxies and warm-input latency, not contemporaneous energy. Exact 25-run configuration/seed/checkpoint/hash registry, transition coverage, selected-policy support and physical action maps are provided as JSON.

\section{Recovery and instrumentation}
Block 1 keeps five accepted runs and a new-numbered Static-LightLR replacement after external-load rejection. Block 5 keeps its accepted runs and new-numbered Cascade idle/workload after gap rejection. Full timelines and replacement/recovery links remain. The second implementation changes only sample-relative waiting and rejected-gap logging; 5\% CPU and 1.5-second gap limits stay fixed. The old rejected right snapshot is absent, so exact failed-gap duration is unknown. Raw guards and ADC logs remain the evidence, not an inferred clean environment.
