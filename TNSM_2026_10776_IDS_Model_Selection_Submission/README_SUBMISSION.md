## Title
When Learned IDS Model Selection Collapses to a Static Choice: A Measurement Study at the Edge

## Structure

The manuscript is organized into eleven sections corresponding to the files under `sections/`:

1. `01_introduction.tex` — Introduction (motivation, framework, four research questions, roadmap) and Fig. 1 (apparatus)
2. `02_background.tex` — Related work across edge IDS, cascades/early exit, inference serving, and learned orchestration
3. `03_system_model.tex` — System model, service objectives, threat model, and adaptive-value criterion
4. `04_data.tex` — Dataset-native data preparation
5. `05_methods.tex` — Detector pool, reward specification, and non-degeneracy framework (Theorem 1, Corollary 1)
6. `06_evaluation.tex` — Evaluation protocol and measurement instrumentation
7. `07_static_results.tex` — Static and attribution results (TON-IoT operating points; static physical; primary; P1A–P1E)
8. `08_dynamic_results.tex` — Dynamic scheduler results (R1 cascade; R2 factorial with DeadlineGuard; R3 stop; R4 threshold diagnostic). Tables III–IV and Fig. 2 are defined at the top of this file so that they float onto the page where R1/R2 are discussed.
9. `09_discussion.tex` — Discussion (attribution, external-budget normalization, support/safety/measurement, practitioner checklist in Sec. IX-C)
10. `10_limitations.tex` — Limitations and reproducibility boundary
11. `11_conclusion.tex` — Conclusion

## Relation to the prior submission

The manuscript is a new submission that fully considers the prior TNSM-2026-10776 reviews. No experiment, split, threshold, policy, or frozen raw measurement was changed.

## Compilation

From this directory:

    pdflatex main.tex && bibtex main && pdflatex main.tex && pdflatex main.tex
    pdflatex supplement.tex && pdflatex supplement.tex
    pdflatex Response_to_Reviewers.tex && pdflatex Response_to_Reviewers.tex
    pdflatex Cover_Letter.tex

A generated `main.bbl`, `IEEEtran.cls`, and `IEEEtran.bst` are included for submission-system portability. The supplement has no bibliography. All figures are under `figures/` and all table rows under `tables/`.

Fig. 1 is built from `figures/experiment_setup_schematic.tex` (TikZ standalone; run `pdflatex` in `figures/`). Fig. 2 and the corrected R2 supplementary plots are generated by `python3 scripts/make_r2_dashboard.py` from `scripts/r2_plot_data.json`, a read-only export of the frozen corrected R2 results. The plotting dependencies are NumPy, Matplotlib and PyMuPDF. The unchanged R1 half of the combined idle-adjusted plot is preserved in `figures/r1_idle_panel_source.pdf`. Generated plots embed TrueType fonts (no Type 3).

The corrected variable-load comparison comprises 60 accepted runs in ten paired blocks. It is separate from the original fixed-load and R1 studies. Supplementary Table S24 retains all three block sets and raw/idle-adjusted endpoints; no historical and corrected measurements are pooled. The plot source and table rows are derived results, not a new training or measurement workflow.

Main-text Tables V and VI use transposed single-column bodies (`tables/r2_key_paired_rows.tex`, `tables/r4_threshold_main_rows.tex`); Supplementary Table S23 still uses `tables/r4_threshold_rows.tex`.

## Reproducibility

The executable audit cited in Section V-D and Supplementary Algorithm S1 is `experiments/adaptive_scheduler_v1/reward_audit.py` in the companion repository. Its deterministic outputs are under `experiments/adaptive_scheduler_v1/reward_audit_validation/`; `python3 reward_audit.py --check` verifies them against the five hash-bound validation traces.
